## Intended service

TrainU provides an organization workspace to upload and review training material and ask questions against approved sources. Availability of features depends on the deployed release and selected configuration. Browser extension use is optional. The present repository does not include integrated payment processing, enterprise SSO or a published extension-store listing.

## Customer responsibilities

Customers designate authorized administrators, manage roles and review source content. They should protect credentials, promptly remove departed members and upload only material they have permission to process/share. Prohibited uses should cover unlawful content, credential theft, unauthorized surveillance, abuse of service limits and attempts to access another tenant's data. The operator must publish its actual suspension/investigation process.

## Answer review and content

AI answers may be incomplete or incorrect. Members should inspect source evidence, especially for consequential decisions. Approval identifies organization-reviewed source material; it does not guarantee every generated statement. The operator should define customer content ownership, the limited processing rights necessary to operate the service and any feedback license in the completed agreement.

## Commercial and exit procedure

A controlled pilot can use a signed scope and manual invoicing. State the actual included seats, supported file/duration envelope, usage limits, support and evaluation period. Do not advertise automatic renewals, refunds, unlimited usage or guaranteed performance unless those are genuinely supported and agreed.

Before termination, agree on export format, authorized recipient, deletion steps, backup expiry and access cut-off. Current data export and organization deletion need operator involvement; automatic retention deletion is not implemented. Publish a working support/contact path for these requests before customer launch.
